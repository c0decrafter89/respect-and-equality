patient, from Latin pati, to suffer, to endure, to undergo — the same root as passion, which originally meant suffering before it meant intense feeling. support, sub- under, and portare, to carry — to carry from below, to bear weight from underneath without being seen. so patience is the willingness to endure alongside someone, and support is doing it from beneath, invisibly, holding weight up rather than pulling from the front.

being patient and supportive means I don't rush her suffering to resolve on my timeline, I endure it alongside her the way pati asks, and I carry what I can from underneath, quietly, not needing credit for the carrying, just being the floor that doesn't give when she needs to stand on something solid.

reframed: sub-pati-carrying — enduring alongside her, and bearing weight invisibly from underneath.
