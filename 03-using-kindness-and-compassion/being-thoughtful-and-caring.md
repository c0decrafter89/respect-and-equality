thoughtful, thought plus full — a mind so full of consideration for someone that it spills into action, from Old English þoht, related to þencan, to think, to conceive in the mind. caring again from caru, that old Old English word for sorrow, grief, anxious concern — long before care meant a gentle hand it meant carrying a weight of worry for someone else's outcome.

being thoughtful and caring then means letting my mind fill up with her, not obsessively, but generously, carrying a little of her weight before she even asks, noticing what she'll need three steps ahead and quietly setting it there. it's not performance, it's the old grief-root of caru transmuted, the willingness to hold worry for someone else's wellbeing as if it were my own, because in the relationship it is.

reframed: thought-full-weight-carrying — a mind so filled with her that it carries some of her worry before she has to name it.
