empathy, from Greek empatheia, en- in, and pathos, feeling, suffering — to feel in, to feel from inside rather than from outside looking at. understanding, under plus stand — to stand underneath, to stand in the midst of, among. so understanding isn't looking down at someone's situation from a comfortable height, it's literally standing under it, letting the weight of what she's carrying press down on me too so I feel the floor she's standing on.

showing empathy and understanding means I don't stay outside her feeling looking in and narrating what she should feel — I climb inside the feeling with her, en-pathos, and I stand under her situation instead of above it, under enough that I feel its floor and its ceiling the way she does. that's the whole difference between sympathy from a distance and actually standing there with her.

reframed: en-pathos-under-standing — feeling from inside rather than describing from outside, standing beneath rather than above.
