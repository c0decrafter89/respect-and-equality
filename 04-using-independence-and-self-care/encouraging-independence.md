encourage, en- into, and cor, heart — to put heart into someone, to infuse courage. independence, in- not, and dependere, to hang from, to be suspended from something else — so independence literally means not-hanging-off. to encourage her independence is to put heart into her not-hanging, to fund the courage it takes to stand on her own legs rather than be suspended from mine.

I think about this and realize encouraging independence isn't stepping back and leaving her alone, it's the opposite — it's actively putting heart into her standing, cheering the muscle it takes to not-hang, to not need me as a crutch, because a relationship between two people who don't need to hang off each other is stronger than one where either of us is suspended.
