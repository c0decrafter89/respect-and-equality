support again, sub-portare, to carry from below. time, Old English tima, related to tide, a stretch, a due season. space, Latin spatium, an extent, a stretch, room to move. so supporting her time and space means carrying, from underneath, the room she needs to have her own due season, her own stretch of extent that isn't filled up with my needs.

practically that looks like protecting the hours she needs alone or with her own people, not as rejection of me but as tide, as season, as the natural stretch a person needs to breathe in. I carry that space for her the way a riverbank carries a river, not constraining it but giving it a shape it can move freely inside of.

reframed: sub-tide-spatium — carrying, from below, the due season and open extent she needs to move freely.
