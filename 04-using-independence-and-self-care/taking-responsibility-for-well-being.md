responsibility, from respondere, to respond, to promise back, re- back and spondere, to pledge — so responsibility is the ability to respond, to pledge back when called on. well-being, wel, in a good manner, and beon, to be — being in a good manner, a good way of being rather than a possession.

taking responsibility for well-being, mine and hers, means building the ability to respond when called, pledging back reliably rather than going silent or defensive, and remembering well-being isn't a finish line but a manner of being, something practiced daily in how I show up, not achieved once and kept.

reframed: re-spondere-wel-beon — pledging back reliably when called, in service of a good manner of being, not a finished state.
