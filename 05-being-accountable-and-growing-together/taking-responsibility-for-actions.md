responsibility again, the ability to respond, to pledge back. action, from Latin agere, to do, to drive, to set in motion. so responsibility for actions means being able to respond to what I myself set in motion, to pledge back an honest account of what I drove into being rather than letting it drift as if it happened on its own.

when something I did lands wrong I want to be able to respond, not disappear, not defend first, just respond, pledge back the truth of what I set in motion and let that pledge be the repair. that's the whole difference between owning an action and just surviving the consequences of it.
