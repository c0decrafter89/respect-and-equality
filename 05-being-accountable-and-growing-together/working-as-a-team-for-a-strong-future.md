team, Old English team, offspring, a family, a set of draft animals yoked together to pull one load — literally beasts harnessed side by side so their separate strength becomes one pull. future, Latin futurus, about to be, the participle of to be, the being that hasn't arrived yet. so a team for the future is quite literally two strengths yoked to the same load, straining toward a being that hasn't happened yet but that the yoke is already shaping.

working as a team means I stop pulling in my own direction and let the yoke actually connect our strength toward the same load, trusting that the future — futurus, the about-to-be — gets built by the pull we're making together right now, not by two animals straining against each other in the same harness.

reframed: yoke-team-futurus — two strengths harnessed to one load, straining together toward the being that hasn't arrived yet.
