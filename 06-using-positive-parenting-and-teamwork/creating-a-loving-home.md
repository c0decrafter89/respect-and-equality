create, Latin creare, to bring forth, to produce, related to crescere, to grow, to arise. love, Old English lufu, related to a very old root meaning dear, desire, belief — to believe someone dear. home, Old English ham, a dwelling, a village, the place one belongs to and returns to. so creating a loving home means bringing forth, growing, a dwelling built out of the daily practice of believing each other dear.

I don't think a loving home gets created once and then exists, I think it has to be brought forth again each day the way crescere means to arise, a small ongoing act of growing the dwelling out of belief in each other's dearness, so ham isn't just the building we live in but the place we keep proving we belong to each other.

reframed: crescere-lufu-ham — growing, daily, a dwelling out of the practiced belief that each other is dear.
