decision, Latin decidere, de- off, and caedere, to cut — a decision is literally a cutting off, the severing of every other option so one path remains. together, gathered, in company. so making decisions together means the cutting off happens with both blades in hand at once, not one person swinging the knife while the other watches the options disappear.

I want every real decision between us to be a shared cut, both of us feeling what gets severed and choosing it on purpose rather than one of us discovering afterward that a door got closed without them, because a decision made alone still cuts off the other person's options even if they never held the knife.

reframed: caedere-gæd — a shared cutting, both hands on the blade when a path gets chosen and the rest let go.
