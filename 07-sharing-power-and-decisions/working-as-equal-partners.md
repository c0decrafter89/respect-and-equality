equal, Latin aequalis, even, level, uniform. partner, from Old French parçonier, one who shares a part, a parcener, from Latin partitionem, a dividing into parts. so equal partners means the parts we've divided the load into are level, even, neither of us holding a heavier share while pretending the ground is flat.

working as equal partners means I actually check the level, notice when my part got lighter and hers got heavier without either of us saying so, and rebalance instead of just calling it equal because we agreed to the word once. equality isn't a title, it's a level you have to keep checking with an actual tool, not a feeling.

reframed: aequalis-parçonier — dividing the load into parts and actually checking the level, not just naming it equal.
