transparent, Latin trans- through, and parere, to appear — to appear through, to let what's behind be seen through the surface. money, from Moneta, an epithet of the Roman goddess Juno, whose temple housed the mint, moneta meaning she who warns or reminds. so being transparent about money means letting the numbers appear through, not hidden behind a surface, and remembering money's old root means to remind — the numbers are meant to remind us of our actual position, not to be hidden so the reminder never lands.

I want the accounts to appear through, no locked surface, no separate hidden ledger, because hiding a moneta, a reminder, defeats its whole purpose — it was never meant to be secret, it was meant to keep reminding both of us where we actually stand.

reframed: trans-parere-moneta — letting the numbers appear through the surface so they can keep reminding us both where we stand.
