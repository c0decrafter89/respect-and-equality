decision, a cutting off, severing other paths. benefit, Latin bene- well, and facere, to do — to do well by. family, Latin familia, a household, everyone under one roof sharing the load of it, from famulus, one who serves the household. so a decision that benefits the family is a cut made in service of doing well by everyone sharing the roof, not a cut made to serve one person's convenience while the rest of the household absorbs it.

when we cut off options together I want to ask who's actually being done well by, bene-facere, and remember the whole point of familia is that everyone under this roof is served by the choice, not just the one holding the knife when it's cut.

reframed: bene-facere-familia — cutting a path in service of doing well by everyone sharing the roof, not just the one deciding.
